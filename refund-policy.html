Refund Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

90-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We stand behind our products with a 90-day money-back guarantee. If you are not completely satisfied with your Cut Signal Pro purchase, we will refund your payment in full.

Eligibility

To be eligible for a refund:

  • Your purchase must have been made within the last 90 days
  • The product must be in its original condition (not damaged by the customer)
  • You must contact us with your order details and reason for return

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund:

  1. Email us at cutsignalpro@gmail.com
  2. Include your full name, order email, and reason for the refund
  3. Our team will review your request within 24-48 hours
  4. If approved, you will receive refund instructions

Refund Processing

Refunds are processed in USDT to the same wallet address used for the original payment. Processing time is typically 24-48 hours after approval. You will receive a confirmation email once the refund has been sent.

Non-Refundable Items

The following are not eligible for refund:

  • Products damaged by the customer (drops, water damage, etc.)
  • Products returned after 90 days from delivery
  • Products without a valid order record

Damaged or Defective Products

If you receive a damaged or defective product, contact us immediately at cutsignalpro@gmail.com with photos of the damage. We will send a replacement at no additional cost or issue a full refund.

Missing Orders

If your order does not arrive within the estimated delivery window (3-9 working days), please contact us. We will investigate with the shipping carrier and either reship your order or provide a full refund.
